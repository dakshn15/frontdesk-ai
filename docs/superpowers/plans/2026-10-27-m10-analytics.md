# Milestone 10: analytics and usage

**Goal:** An Analytics page that shows, for a date range:

- conversations, bookings made by the AI, leads and the handoff rate;
- resolution without a human;
- top topics;
- questions the receptionist couldn't answer well;
- AI usage and estimated cost per day by provider.

Quiet conversations are tagged with a topic and an outcome.

**Spec:** [4.9 Analytics](../specs/2026-10-24-frontdesk-ai-design.md), and the cost lines in sections 5 and 6.

**Architecture:**
- **Cost** is an estimate from a price table in the code, applied to `usage_events` token counts.
- **Tagging:** a conversation is tagged once it's been quiet for 30 minutes.
  - The topic comes from a fixed list, by rules over the tools used and the visitor's words.
  - The outcome comes from what happened: booked, lead, handed off, answered or unanswered.
  - Tagging is deterministic, explainable and free.
  - It runs on each Analytics page load (bounded), and daily from the cron for all workspaces.
- **Bookings** made from a widget card record their conversation.
- **Queries** run under RLS. Charts use Recharts.
