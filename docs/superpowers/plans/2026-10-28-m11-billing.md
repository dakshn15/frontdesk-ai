# Milestone 11: Stripe billing and limits

**Goal:** Workspaces start on a 14-day trial, then subscribe to Starter or Pro through Stripe Checkout, and manage their plan in Stripe's Customer Portal. Each plan limits:

- website chats a month;
- knowledge sources;
- team seats.

At a chat limit, or after an expired trial, the widget offers the contact form instead of AI replies, and the owner is emailed. Nothing is deleted.

**Spec:** [4.10 Billing](../specs/2026-10-24-frontdesk-ai-design.md); Stripe events are stored for idempotency (section 7).

**Architecture:**

- **Stripe REST** through `fetch`, with no SDK: `src/privileged/billing/stripe.ts`.
- **Webhooks** at `/api/webhooks/stripe`: a signature check (HMAC SHA-256 with a 5-minute tolerance), then `stripe_events` for idempotency, then `subscriptions` synced from `customer.subscription.*` events.
- **The effective plan** is a pure function of the subscription row, when the workspace was created, whether it's a demo, and now. A demo sandbox is unlimited, since demos have their own caps.

## Tasks

1. **Plans, limits and webhooks.**
   - Migration: `subscriptions` (one per workspace) and `stripe_events`; members read subscriptions, and only the server writes.
   - `src/features/billing/plans.ts`: `PLANS` and `effectivePlan`.
   - `src/privileged/billing/`:
     - `limits.ts`: `chatAllowed`, `canAddKnowledge`, `canAddSeat`, and the limit email;
     - `stripe.ts`: checkout, the portal, `verifySignature`;
     - `webhook.ts`: `handleStripeWebhook(request, secret)`.
   - Enforcement:
     - `runAgent` (live chats) checks `chatAllowed`;
     - adding knowledge and inviting someone check their limits.
   - Tests:
     - unit tests for plans and the signature check;
     - integration tests for the webhook (a signed event, a repeated event, a bad signature), chat limits, trial expiry, the knowledge and seat limits, and RLS.
2. **The Billing page** (owners and admins):
   - the plan, trial days left and usage meters;
   - plan cards with Subscribe and Manage billing;
   - a dashboard banner when the trial has ended or the chat limit is reached;
   - env: `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`, `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, `STRIPE_PRICE_STARTER`, `STRIPE_PRICE_PRO`.
   - E2E: the Billing page shows the trial, the usage and the plans, with "Billing isn't set up yet" when there are no keys.
