# Milestone 7: inbox and live takeover

**Goal:** The team sees every website conversation in an inbox that updates live. They can take a chat over from the AI, reply in the visitor's widget (or by email when the visitor left their address), hand it back, or resolve it. Handoffs alert managers by email and in the dashboard.

**Spec:** [4.7 Inbox and live takeover](../specs/2026-10-24-frontdesk-ai-design.md).

**Architecture:**

- Staff actions run under RLS. New column grants and policies let members update a conversation's status and insert `staff` messages written as themselves.
- `conversations` and `messages` join the `supabase_realtime` publication. The dashboard subscribes with the signed-in user's client, so Realtime only sends rows the user can read, then refreshes the server-rendered page.
- The widget polls `/api/widget/history` every 4 seconds, and only while the conversation is waiting for or handled by a person.

## Global constraints

- Copy is plain, with no exclamation marks.
- Migrations go to production before the code.
- TDD for the logic, one e2e run at the end, in CI mode.
- No new dependencies.

## Tasks

1. **Takeover rules.**
   - Migration:
     - `conversations.assigned_to` and `handoff_at`, plus `messages.author_id`;
     - an update policy for members on conversations, and an insert policy on messages (role `staff`, `author_id = auth.uid()`, member);
     - column grants;
     - the realtime publication.
   - `src/features/inbox/records.ts` (all records return a status string):
     - `takeOverRecord`: `ok`, or `taken` when someone else already has it;
     - `handBackRecord` and `resolveRecord`;
     - `replyRecord`, which only works while a person is handling the chat.
   - `src/features/inbox/open-now.ts`: `isOpenAt(hours, closedDates, timeZone, now)`.
   - The `handoff_to_human` tool sets `handoff_at` and emails owners and admins once per handoff (`src/privileged/inbox-emails.ts`).
   - A visitor message on a resolved chat reopens it for the AI.
   - Staff replies can be emailed to the conversation's lead address (looked up on the server, never typed by the user).
   - Tests: integration tests for records and RLS, the alert email, and reopening; unit tests for `isOpenAt`.
2. **Inbox pages.**
   - `/w/[slug]/inbox`:
     - status tabs with counts (All, Needs a person, Team, AI, Resolved);
     - search over messages and lead name or email;
     - a list with a preview, the visitor, the status and the time.
   - `/w/[slug]/inbox/[id]`:
     - a timeline of messages and the tools the AI used, with sources;
     - the lead's details;
     - Take over, Hand back, Resolve;
     - a reply box with "Also email this reply".
   - The nav gets an Inbox entry for every member, with a badge counting chats that need a person.
   - `LiveUpdates` (client): the inbox pages refresh on any change. Every workspace page shows a toast with a link when a chat starts needing a person.
3. **The widget picks up staff replies.**
   - History returns:
     - staff messages with the author's first name;
     - `askForEmail`: waiting for a person, and either 5 minutes have passed or the business is closed now, and no lead has been left yet.
   - The widget polls while the chat is waiting for or handled by a person. It appends new staff messages and shows "You're chatting with Ana from the team". When `askForEmail` is set, it shows the lead form.
   - The scripted model hands off when the visitor asks for a person.
   - E2E: the visitor asks for a person, the inbox shows it live, staff take over and reply, and the visitor sees the reply.

## Review focus

- A staff member replying while someone else handles the chat: allowed, since the team shares the inbox. Taking over from someone else: refused with a message.
- Emailing a reply to an address the user typed: impossible, because the address comes from the conversation's own lead.
- Realtime leaking another workspace's rows: RLS applies to Realtime, and the subscription filters by workspace.
- Polling forever: it stops once the chat is back with the AI or resolved, or the tab is hidden.
- The handoff alert sent twice for one handoff: only the transition from `ai` or `resolved` to `needs_human` sends it.
